## Practical limits

The Gherkin helper validates fences, scenario IDs, executable Given/When/Then coverage, literal arguments and populated Outline examples. It is not a full Gherkin compiler: it does not prove every column/placeholder binding or business completeness.

Independent contexts and actual logs matter; a declared actor identifier, verifier option, digest or passing test count alone cannot establish sound reasoning or truthful execution. Qualification keeps that distinction explicit.
